AuraFitness Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

AuraFitness aims to provide a usable experience across iPhone, iPad, Android, and web. We continue refining readability, focus states, and tap targets as the product evolves.

Current accessibility work

  • Visible labels and direct references for nutrition and health-related content.
  • Large tap targets for primary actions on mobile.
  • Improved route wiring so interactive controls do not lead to dead ends.

Need help using the app?

Email support@aurafitness.org and describe the issue, device, and app version so we can investigate.
